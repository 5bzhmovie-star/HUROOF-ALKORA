Flags cover available ISO flags plus football country aliases (ENG/SCO/WAL and country names). National teams appear in search. Picking a player fills its actual recorded flag and club logo. Team selection lists dated source lineups and roster cards; exact starting XI only comes from a recorded match, never arbitrary alphabetical player selection. Existing historical lineup references receive explicit slots and known positions with cited facts, retaining missing portraits as missing. Users can create and store additional dated lineups for any team from the editor; later name selection fills them.

Coach careers use club/national-team stations and coach portrait. Common-link has exactly 4 named photo cards and private shared answer; odd-one-out has 4 cards, one private answer index and private explanation; missing-player is an 11-player positional lineup with one answer slot hidden until reveal. Author selects that slot and answer/letter follows. All published media must exist locally. Incomplete drafts can be saved and cannot be drawn. Existing questions survive migration.
